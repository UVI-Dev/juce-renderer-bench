## RepaintRepro, 1,200 widgets

Frame rate and paint time (RepaintRepro's own output, labels not cached):

| Load | Engine | fps | Paint |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 areas repainting | CoreGraphics | ~30 (each repaint covers the whole window) | ~18 ms per repaint |
| 2 areas repainting | Metal | 60 | < 1 ms per frame |
| Whole window | CoreGraphics | ~34 | 15.5 ms per repaint |
| Whole window | Metal | 60 | 12.2 ms per frame (text layout, see below) |

With labels cached (`--textcache`), whole window: both at 60 fps, paint 5.1 ms (CoreGraphics) vs 2.9 ms (Metal).

Total CPU+GPU power of the machine (`scripts/power_compare.sh --textcache`, 10 one-second samples, median where a few samples spike; idle ~0.15 W):

| Load | CoreGraphics | Metal |
|---|---|---|
| 2 areas repainting | ~1.3 W | ~0.2-0.3 W |
| 8 areas repainting | ~1.35 W | ~0.25 W |
| Whole window | ~2.1 W | ~1.45 W |

Without the label cache, `Graphics::drawText` re-lays out every label on every frame (its arrangement cache holds 128 entries) and takes ~90% of the message thread with any engine.
