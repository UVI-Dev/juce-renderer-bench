# Results: M1 MacBook Pro, macOS 13.7, 60 Hz display (2026-11-07)

JUCE 9.0.5 with the Metal renderer branch (commit f7afba98af: windows presented through their own
CAMetalLayer, paint() only records), Release builds. "Metal before" is the previous version of the
branch (CAMetalLayer tiles, paths and new glyphs prepared inside paint()).

## ComponentDiagnosticsDemo (`scripts/diagnostics_sweep.sh`)

All 54 examples, the three engines in one session. "Paint" is the demo's "total (median)": the time
spent in paint() on the message thread. With the CoreGraphics renderer, paint() only records and
the rasterisation happens on a Core Animation thread, so look at the process columns too
(milliseconds per second of wall time).

| Engine | Paint, median | Paint, mean | App CPU | App GPU | WindowServer CPU | WindowServer GPU |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| CoreGraphics | 0.051 ms | 1.43 ms | 317 | 55 | 272 | 34 |
| Metal before | 0.100 ms | 1.42 ms | 280 | 61 | 279 | 35 |
| Metal | 0.027 ms | 1.24 ms | 270 | 65 | 277 | 35 |

Paint in ms for the examples reported as slower on the forum:

| Example | CoreGraphics | Metal before | Metal |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Complex path (not-cached) | 0.038 | 0.075 | 0.008 |
| Complex path (cached) | 0.046 | 0.116 | 0.003 |
| Complex path (with rounded corners) | 0.119 | 1.188 | 0.035 |
| Graphics demo (Shapes) | 0.089 | 0.072 | 0.013 |
| Graphics demo (Lines) | 0.060 | 0.052 | 0.018 |
| Font (animated transform) | 0.024 | 0.902 | 0.029 |
| Widgets demo (*) | 0.99 | 0.77 | 0.61 |

(*) Widgets' readings move a lot with what is animating at that moment: median of several runs
alternating the engines (`scripts/diagnostics_ab.sh`, 4 rounds, 6 readings per run). App CPU on
that example: ~320 ms/s with CoreGraphics, ~225 before, ~221 now.

Fullscreen ComponentDiagnosticsDemo: 60 fps steady with Metal (the previous tiled version dropped
to 1-5 fps).

## RepaintRepro, 1,200 widgets, labels cached (`--textcache`)

CPU + GPU time of the app and WindowServer, ms per second, average of 2 passes, all at 60 fps:

| Load | CoreGraphics | Metal |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2 areas repainting | 891 | 715 |
| 8 areas repainting | 914 | 757 |
| Whole window | 1495 | 957 |

With 2 or 8 small areas CoreGraphics repaints their bounding box, here the whole window.
